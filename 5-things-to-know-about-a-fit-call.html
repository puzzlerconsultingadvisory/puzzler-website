5 Things to Know About a Fit Call

Thirty minutes. No pitch, no commitment. You’ll leave with a clear sense of fit and one specific suggestion you can act on.

Swipe or scroll sideways to see all five slides.

Prefer to read the five points?
  1. It is a conversation. A Fit Call is not a sales presentation. It is an honest conversation about where you are, what is changing and what kind of support would be useful.
  2. Come with the real challenge. You do not need to arrive with a perfectly defined problem. Tell me what feels stuck, unclear, disconnected or harder than it should be.
  3. Expect questions. Good consulting begins with listening. I may ask about your goals, people, processes, timing and what you have already tried.
  4. Fit works both ways. You are deciding whether you trust my approach. I am determining whether Puzzler has the right experience, capacity and services to help you move forward.
  5. There is no pressure. A Fit Call may lead to an engagement, a referral or simply greater clarity about your next step. The goal is not to force a piece into place. It is to discover what truly fits.
